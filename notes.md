GPT‑6 Astra is OpenAI’s latest flagship model, launched on September 3, 2026 and described by the company as “the world’s most intelligent and aligned model.”  It is positioned as a generational leap in capability, especially for agentic, computer‑use, coding, and professional workloads. [openai](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)

## Core capabilities

Astra is state‑of‑the‑art on computer and browser use, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and complex professional tasks.  It scores 72.6% on OSWorld 2.0 (computer‑use benchmark) while completing tasks about 47% faster than its predecessor GPT‑5.6 Sol, and it saturates FrontierMath Tier 4 (~97–98%), ARC‑AGI‑3 (99.9%), and ExploitBench (100%).  OpenAI also highlights Astra’s improved judgment and alignment, making it more reliable when delegating multi‑step workflows. [openai](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)

## Technical specs and access

Astra has a 1.05‑million‑token context window, up to 128k output tokens, and an April 30, 2026 knowledge cutoff.  It is available first to selected organizations, then to ChatGPT Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise users, and via the API as `gpt-6-astra` (and on Azure/AWS).  Standard API pricing is $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, with a faster “fast mode” at roughly 2× the price.  OpenAI has temporarily paused new $200 Pro tier sign‑ups due to demand and infrastructure constraints. [openai](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)

## Professional focus

Beyond general chat, Astra underpins new products like ChatGPT for Financial Services and is paired with GPT‑Live‑1 for full‑duplex voice agents, signaling a shift toward embedding high‑level reasoning directly into professional workflows.
